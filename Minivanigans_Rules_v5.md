# MINIVANIGANS!
## Ordinary Characters. Extraordinary Shifts.
### Rules v5.0 — Shift Playtest


## The Big Idea

Minivanigans! is a make-your-own card game about ordinary characters having extraordinary
moments. Pack your Minivan with family members, friends, pets, imaginary creatures, and
everyday oddballs—then give everyone a chance to unleash something spectacular.

Every Character has two moves:

- An **Everyday Move** that can be used again and again.
- A more powerful **Shift Move** that becomes spent after use.

On every turn, you face one central choice:

> **Take an Action and use an Everyday Move—or pass your Action and unleash a Shift Move.**

Characters recover their Shift Moves by resting in your **Minivan**. Winning means knowing when
to stay, when to Shift, and when to rotate a new Character into the spotlight.

The first player to score **3 Knockouts** wins.

---

## What You Need

Each player needs:

- One legal **18-card deck**
- A few counters, coins, or small dice for tracking damage
- Three tokens for tracking Knockouts
- A coin or another fair way to choose the first player

No separate energy cards are used.

## Important Terms

- **Active:** The Character currently in front, using moves and taking damage.
- **Minivan:** Up to three face-up Characters waiting behind your Active.
- **Ready:** A Character whose Shift Move is available. Ready Characters are upright.
- **Spent:** A Character that has used its Shift Move. Turn it sideways.
- **Moment:** A one-use card played as your Action.
- **Knockout:** When a Character has damage equal to or greater than its Stamina.
- **KO Star:** A token showing that you scored a Knockout.

# 1. The Cards

## Character Cards

Every Character card displays:

- A custom Character name and picture
- Its standard **Chassis**
- Its **Stamina**
- An **Everyday Move**
- A **Shift Move**
- Optional flavor text

Stamina is how much damage a Character can take before being Knocked Out.

### The Real-Effect Rule

You may rename your Character, Everyday Move, and Shift Move, but the standard chassis and
effect must always appear beneath the custom name.

Example:

**Burnt Espresso Blast**

*The Cannon — Big Finish: 4 damage.*

Players should never have to guess what a custom card does.

## Moment Cards

Moments are one-use cards representing the strange interruptions, lucky breaks, and minor
disasters that happen during the day.

Playing a Moment uses your Action. After resolving it, place it face-up in your discard pile.

Moment cards may also be renamed, but their standard name and full effect must remain visible.

## No Mechanical Classes or Rarities

Version 5 has no class-advantage triangle. Characters do not deal extra damage because of a
class matchup.

Words such as Shifter, Stray, NPC, Common, or Premium may still appear as flavor or cosmetic
labels, but they have no rules effect.

# 2. Character Chassis

Choose one of these eight chassis when making a Character. Its Stamina and standard effects
cannot be changed during normal deck building.

| Chassis | Stamina | Everyday Move | Shift Move | Play Style |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **The Anchor** | 7 | **Steady Hit:** 2 damage | **Not Today:** 3 damage. Shield 1. | Durable defender |
| **The Cannon** | 4 | **Big Hit:** 3 damage | **Big Finish:** 4 damage. | Fragile attacker |
| **The Pivot** | 5 | **Quick Hit:** 2 damage | **Keep Moving:** 2 damage. You may switch afterward. | Flexible rotation |
| **The Sustainer** | 6 | **Keep Going:** 2 damage | **Second Snack:** 2 damage, then heal 2 from this Character. | Healing |
| **The Trickster** | 5 | **Pester:** 2 damage | **Throw Off the Plan:** 2 damage, then spend the opposing Active. | Disruption |
| **The Captain** | 6 | **Lead the Way:** 2 damage | **Rally the Van:** 3 damage, then ready one Character in your Minivan. | Team support |
| **The Wildcard** | 5 | **Surprise Hit:** 2 damage | **Anything Could Happen:** Choose one Wildcard effect below. | Adaptability |
| **The Underdog** | 5 | **Try Again:** 2 damage | **Against the Odds:** 3 damage, or 4 if your opponent has more KO Stars. | Comeback attacker |

### Chassis Effect Reference

#### Shield 1

The next time that Character would take damage before the start of your next turn, reduce that
damage by 1. A Character can have only one Shield at a time. Use a token to remember it.

Remove the Shield after it reduces damage or at the start of your next turn, whichever happens
first.

#### Switch Afterward

After damage and Knockouts are resolved, you may exchange your Active with one Character in
your Minivan. This switch is free and does not change the move that was already used.

If your Active was Knocked Out during the move's resolution, it cannot switch.

#### Heal

Remove that much damage from the Character. Damage can never go below zero.

#### Spend the Opposing Active

Turn the opposing Active sideways. If it was already spent, nothing additional happens.

Moving that Character to the Minivan allows it to recover normally.

#### Ready a Minivan Character

Turn one spent Character in your Minivan upright. It is now ready.

#### The Wildcard's Shift

When using **Anything Could Happen**, choose one:

1. Deal **3 damage**.
2. Deal **2 damage**, then heal 1 from the Wildcard.
3. Deal **2 damage**, then you may switch.

Announce the choice before dealing damage.

# 3. Building a Deck

A legal deck contains exactly **18 cards**:

- Exactly **12 Characters**
- Exactly **6 Moments**

Deck limits:

- No more than **3 Characters with the same chassis**
- No more than the printed number of copies of each Moment
- Custom names do not bypass a copy limit

For example, three differently named Cannons still count as three copies of The Cannon.

## Making a Character

Creating a Character takes three steps:

1. Pick one of the eight chassis.
2. Create its name, art, move names, and flavor.
3. Print the standard chassis and effects clearly on the card.

This keeps homemade decks fair while allowing every Character to feel personal.

# 4. Moment Menu

| Moment | Effect | Maximum |
|---|---|---:|
| **Snack Break** | Heal 2 from your Active. | 2 |
| **Running Late** | Switch. Your new Active's Everyday Move deals +1 damage this turn. | 2 |
| **Carpool** | Put one Character from your hand into an open Minivan space. You may switch to that Character. | 2 |
| **Detour** | Your opponent switches their Active with a Minivan Character of their choice, if able. | 1 |
| **Encore** | Ready your Active. It may use its Shift Move this turn even though playing Encore used your Action. | 1 |
| **Group Chat** | Search your deck for one Character, reveal it, put it into your hand, then shuffle. | 2 |
| **Backseat Driver** | After your Everyday Move this turn, you may switch. | 2 |
| **Good Vibes** | Heal 1 from up to two different Characters you control. | 2 |

### Moment Clarifications

- A Moment still uses your one Action even if part of its effect cannot happen.
- **Running Late:** You must have a Character in your Minivan to play it. Its +1 applies only
  to an Everyday Move used by the Character that switched in.
- **Carpool:** You must have an open Minivan space and a Character in your hand to play it.
- **Detour:** If the opponent's Minivan is empty, Detour cannot be played.
- **Encore:** This is a deliberate exception to the normal rule. The Active may Shift after
  the Action even though it normally could use only its Everyday Move.
- **Backseat Driver:** Resolve damage and any Knockout before switching.
- **Good Vibes:** You may choose the Active and/or Characters in your Minivan. You cannot
  choose the same Character twice.

# 5. Game Setup

1. Choose the first player with a coin flip.
2. Each player shuffles their deck.
3. The first player draws **5 cards**. The second player draws **6 cards**.
4. If a player has no Character in their opening hand, they may shuffle the hand into their
   deck and redraw the same number. They may do this up to two times.
5. If a player still has no Character, reveal cards from the top of their deck until a
   Character appears. Put that Character into their hand and place the other revealed cards
   on the bottom of the deck in any order.
6. Both players secretly choose one Character from their hand, then reveal them together as
   their Active Characters.
7. Each player may place up to **3 additional Characters** from their hand face-up in their
   Minivan. Initial Minivan placement is free.
8. Put the remaining cards in hand. Set out three spaces for KO Stars.
9. All Characters begin ready and with no damage.

The first player draws normally at the start of their first turn.

### Opening-Turn Rule

On the first turn of the game, the first player may use only an Everyday Move. This is true
even if they pass their Action or play Encore.

After that first turn, all normal rules apply.

# 6. Taking a Turn

Every turn has five steps:

1. **Ready the Minivan**
2. **Draw**
3. **Action or Pass**
4. **Move**
5. **End**

## Step 1: Ready the Minivan

Turn every spent Character in your Minivan upright.

Your spent Active does not ready automatically. To recharge it, you normally need to move it
to the Minivan.

Remove any expired Shields from your Characters.

## Step 2: Draw

Draw one card.

If your deck is empty, skip the draw. Running out of cards is not a loss.

## Step 3: Take One Action or Pass

Choose exactly one of the following:

### Bench

Place one Character from your hand face-up into an open Minivan space.

Your Minivan holds no more than three Characters.

### Switch

Exchange your Active with one Character in your Minivan.

Damage and ready/spent status stay on both Characters. The Character sent to the Minivan will
ready at the start of your next turn.

### Play a Moment

Play one Moment from your hand, follow its text, then discard it.

### Pass

Do not take an Action. Passing is how you gain access to a normal Shift Move during Step 4.

## Step 4: Use a Move

Your Active may use one move:

- If you took an Action, it may use only its **Everyday Move**.
- If you passed, it may use its **Everyday Move** or its ready **Shift Move**.
- A spent Character cannot use its Shift Move.
- **Encore** creates the one standard exception.

Using a move is optional, but a move that has been announced must finish resolving.

### Using an Everyday Move

Follow its damage and effect. The Character remains ready or spent exactly as it was.

### Using a Shift Move

Announce the Shift Move and turn the Character sideways immediately. Then resolve its damage
and effect in the order printed.

The Shift is spent once announced, even if part of its effect becomes impossible.

## Step 5: End

Your maximum hand size is **7 cards**. If you have more than 7, choose and discard down to 7.

Then the other player begins their turn.

# 7. Damage and Knockouts

## Dealing Damage

Place damage counters on the opposing Active equal to the move's damage.

Damage stays on a Character when it switches to the Minivan. Switching does not heal.

Apply Shield reductions before placing damage.

## Checking for a Knockout

After a move deals damage, check whether the defending Character has damage equal to or greater
than its Stamina.

If so, resolve these steps in order:

1. Put the Knocked Out Character face-up in its owner's discard pile.
2. The attacking player gains **1 KO Star**.
3. If the attacking player now has 3 KO Stars, they win immediately.
4. Otherwise, the defending player chooses a new Active:
   - First, choose any Character from their Minivan.
   - If the Minivan is empty, choose a Character from their hand.
   - If neither contains a Character, reveal cards from the deck until a Character appears.
     It becomes the new Active; put the other revealed cards on the bottom of the deck.
5. The new Active keeps any damage and ready/spent status it already had.

Choosing a replacement is free. It does not use the defending player's next Action.

If a player must choose a new Active and has no Characters in their Minivan, hand, or deck,
they lose immediately.

## Effects After Damage

Unless a card says otherwise:

1. Deal damage.
2. Resolve the defending Character's Knockout.
3. Resolve any “afterward” effect, such as healing or switching.

An effect that refers to the opposing Active means the Character that was attacked, not a
replacement sent out after a Knockout. The effect does not carry over to the replacement.

An effect cannot heal, spend, or switch a Character that was already Knocked Out.

---

# 8. Winning

The first player to gain **3 KO Stars** wins.

You also win if your opponent must produce a new Active and has no Characters remaining in
their Minivan, hand, or deck.

An empty deck does not cause a loss and does not create sudden death. Continue playing and
skip empty draws until someone wins normally.

KO Stars cannot be removed in version 5.

# 9. Example Turn

Maya's Active is **Cafeteria Grandma**, built with The Sustainer chassis. It has taken 3 damage
and its Shift Move is ready.

Maya considers three choices:

- Play **Snack Break**, heal 2, then use the 2-damage Everyday Move.
- Switch to a fresh Character and use that new Character's Everyday Move.
- Pass the Action, use **Second Snack**, deal 2 damage, heal 2, and spend Cafeteria Grandma.

Maya passes and uses the Shift Move. She turns Cafeteria Grandma sideways, deals 2 damage, and
heals 2.

On Maya's next turn, Cafeteria Grandma is still spent because it remained Active. Maya may:

- Use its Everyday Move,
- Switch it into the Minivan so it can recover, or
- Use Encore to ready it and Shift again.

That is the central rhythm of Minivanigans!

# 10. First Game Mode

Use this short tutorial for brand-new or very young players.

1. Remove all Moment cards.
2. Each player chooses 3 Characters instead of using a deck.
3. Put one Character Active and two in the Minivan.
4. Skip drawing, hands, and benching.
5. On a turn, either:
   - Switch and use an Everyday Move, or
   - Do not switch and use an Everyday or ready Shift Move.
6. Spent Characters still recover in the Minivan.
7. The first player to score **2 Knockouts** wins.

Do not remove Shift Moves from the teaching game. Shifting is the part new players should learn
first.

After one tutorial game, use the complete rules.

# 12. Customization and Fair Play

The purpose of Minivanigans! is to make cards that feel like they belong to the people playing.

Good customization includes:

- Family members, friends, pets, imaginary creatures, or silly everyday characters
- Hand-drawn, photographed, collaged, or generated artwork
- Funny move names that match the standard effect
- Flavor text and cosmetic badges

## Content Rule

Card names, art, and text must be appropriate for everyone participating. A group may reject a
card containing hateful, sexual, threatening, or otherwise inappropriate material.

## Cosmetic Rewards

Game-night trophies, foil stars, signatures, and “MVP” marks should remain cosmetic. Winning
should never permanently make a card mechanically stronger.

# 13. Quick Reference

## Setup

- Deck: 12 Characters + 6 Moments
- First player: 5 cards
- Second player: 6 cards
- 1 Active + up to 3 face-up Minivan Characters
- First to 3 KO Stars

## Turn

1. Ready spent Minivan Characters.
2. Draw 1.
3. Take one Action or pass.
4. Action taken: Everyday Move only.
5. Action passed: Everyday Move or ready Shift Move.
6. Discard down to 7.

## Recharge

- Using a Shift Move turns the Active sideways.
- A spent Active does not ready on its own.
- Move it to the Minivan.
- At the start of your next turn, spent Minivan Characters ready.

## Opening Player

The first player uses only an Everyday Move on the first turn of the game.
